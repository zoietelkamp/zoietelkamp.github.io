Research Experience

  • Graduate Student Researcher
    University of Virginia, Department of Astronomy
    Investigating star formation from low to high masses using observations from ALMA, JWST, Herschel, Spitzer, and SOFIA. Co-developed open-source analysis tools including the sedcreator Python package to enable community science (15+ publications).
  • Junior Astronomy Specialist
    University of California, Berkeley
    Led development of an autoencoder neural network to generate synthetic protoplanetary disk images 1000x faster than traditional radiative transfer calculations, enabling efficient disk modeling. Published first-authored paper in The Astrophysical Journal.
  • Undergraduate Astronomy Researcher
    University of California, Berkeley
    Used methods including principal component analysis to investigate relationships between protoplanetary disk physical properties and morphology. Co-authored two publications and participated in observing runs at Keck and Lick observatories.
