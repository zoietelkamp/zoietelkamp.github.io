Research Experience

  • Investigating Protostellar Outflows with JWST NIRCam
    Graduate Dissertation Research, University of Virginia (2025–Present)
    Complex Organic Molecules in Protostars with ALMA Spectral Surveys (COMPASS) Team
    • Reduced JWST NIRCam images and characterized >30 protostellar outflows in eight star-forming regions; now comparing outflow orientations with magnetic field directions from archival dust polarization data.
    • Building an interactive tool for viewing ALMA and JWST spectra for the COMPASS website.
  • An Archival ALMA Analysis of Disks in Massive Star-Forming Regions
    Graduate Dissertation Research, University of Virginia (2023–2026)
    Advisors/Mentors: A. Plunkett, J. C. Tan
    • Identified and characterized >100 candidate protostellar disks using high-resolution archival ALMA continuum images of 17 massive star-forming regions.
    • Compared disk orientations to literature outflow orientations and performed complementary IR spectral energy distribution (SED) fitting to test massive star formation model predictions (Telkamp et al. 2026, submitted).
  • The SOFIA Massive (SOMA) Star Formation Survey
    Graduate Dissertation Research, University of Virginia (2021–2025)
    Advisors/Mentors: R. Fedriani, J. C. Tan
    • Characterized 34 protostars in crowded Galactic star-forming regions via SOFIA, Spitzer, and Herschel photometry and SED fitting, nearly doubling the size of the survey.
    • Added empirical constraints and ran restricted SED fits of SOMA Paper I–V protostars to test the environmental dependence of massive star formation (Telkamp et al. 2025).
    • Led development of version 2.0 of the open-source Python package sedcreator (Fedriani et al. 2023, Telkamp et al. 2025; used in 15+ publications), adding tools for photometry and SED fitting in crowded star-forming regions.
  • Benchmarking Large Language Models for Astronomical Data Access
    Additional Graduate Research, NSF-Simons AI Institute for Cosmic Origins (CosmicAI) (Summer 2026)
    Advisors/Mentors: A. Plunkett, B. Mason, S. Offner, S. Juneau, R. Nikutta, R. Loomis
    • Built AstroDataBench, an open-source benchmarking framework for evaluating LLM reliability in facilitating archival astronomy data access, in collaboration with NRAO and NOIRLab astronomers (currently being expanded by a CosmicAI intern).
  • Machine Learning Analysis of Edge-On Protoplanetary Disks
    Undergraduate and Post-Baccalaureate Research, University of California, Berkeley (2018–2021)
    Advisor: G. Duchêne
    • Led a project developing a machine learning framework (autoencoder and random forest regression) to generate synthetic edge-on protoplanetary disk images 1000× faster than radiative transfer calculations, to facilitate efficient disk modeling (Telkamp et al. 2022).
    • Developed morphological analysis methods for HST protoplanetary disk images (Villenave et al. 2020) and participated in observing runs at Keck and Lick observatories.
