This project investigates the innermost scales where disks form (~10–100 astronomical units) to search for signatures of either the core accretion process that low-mass stars form through, or competitive accretion in dense protoclusters. Using high-resolution archival radio-wavelength data from the ALMA observatory, we measured disk properties for over 100 massive protostars
