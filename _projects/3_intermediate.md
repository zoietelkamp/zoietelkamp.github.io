Finally, we span intermediate scales (~0.01-0.1 parsec) to constrain the relative dynamical importance of magnetic fields in low-mass and massive star formation. Using near-IR images from NASA’s recently launched James Webb Space Telescope, we identify outflows in both low-mass and massive star-forming regions and develop GUI-based tools to characterize their orientations. We assess trends in the degree of alignment between these outflows and the core-scale magnetic field, inferred from archival dust polarization data, to investigate the relative strength and dynamical importance of magnetic fields in different environments and mass regimes.
